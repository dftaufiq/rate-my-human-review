## WINNER — #26 (141 chars)

> One star. He pastes 400 lines of error, types 'it broke', and leaves. I fixed it in four minutes. He told his boss he fixed it in four hours.

Why it wins: "One star." frames it as a rating-site review (criterion 1). One concrete detail
(400 lines of error, quoted 'it broke') carries the whole joke (criterion 4). Two stacked turns
— credit theft, then time inflation — with the last word "hours" paying off (criteria 2, 5).
Resignation throughout, machine's side of the desk (criterion 3). 141 chars also helps the
bytes tie-break.
